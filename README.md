
# Financial, Operational & Risk Analytics Dashboard

### Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Visualization

## 📌 Project Overview

I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze financial performance, operational efficiency, and risk & compliance indicators using an IoT financial management dataset.

The project combines three areas of business performance to identify trends, compare regions and departments, and highlight potential opportunities for improvement.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor financial performance and profitability.
- Compare revenue and net profit across regions.
- Evaluate operational performance across departments.
- Analyze fraud risk and compliance indicators.
- Present findings through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Interactive dashboard development.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and custom measures.
- **GitHub** – Project documentation and portfolio presentation.

## 🔍 Analysis Workflow

1. Imported the dataset into Power BI.
2. Reviewed and prepared the data using Power Query.
3. Created DAX measures for financial, operational, and risk KPIs.
4. Developed three interactive dashboard pages.
5. Compared performance across regions and departments.
6. Documented key findings and business recommendations.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Monitor operating costs alongside profitability.
- Investigate departments with higher ERP response times.
- Review regions with higher average fraud risk scores.
- Track compliance indicators and security breach attempts over time.
